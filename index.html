About

Senior backend engineer with 8+ years of experience building reliable systems across marketplaces, fintech, and industrial software. My strongest stack is C#/.NET, PostgreSQL, and Kafka, with a focus on distributed systems, event-driven architecture, and production reliability.

I turn unclear business requirements into services with clear responsibilities, explicit data ownership, and well-defined contracts. My work includes monolith-to-microservices migrations, shared messaging and observability libraries, and payment integrations where consistency and failure handling matter. I also served as a core contributor and primary maintainer of Telegram.Bot, a widely adopted .NET library.

Agentic development and AI-assisted system design are part of my everyday practice. I use agents to investigate codebases, compare architectural approaches, develop technical specifications, and review failure scenarios. I define the requirements, service boundaries, contracts, and acceptance criteria, then break the design into focused implementation tasks.

I delegate implementation to coding agents and guide delivery through code review, testing, and iteration. I remain responsible for technical decisions, understanding the generated code, and verifying the result against the intended behavior. Clear specifications and independent validation are central to this workflow.

I’m open to other backend ecosystems, including Go, bringing my existing engineering experience while learning the tools and conventions of a new stack.

Outside work, I play guitar and enjoy experimenting with music and sound.
